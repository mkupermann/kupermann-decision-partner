# Decision brief

**Decision:** The choice and its scope.
**Owner / status:** Who has authority; proposed, decided or superseded.
**Recommendation:** Preferred action and the conditions that make it appropriate.

## Basis

- **Criteria and constraints:** Include the source of material preferences and weights.
- **Options:** Preferred option, strongest alternative, and current approach where relevant.
- **Evidence:** The few facts or calculations carrying the recommendation, with sources and scope.
- **Trade-offs:** Costs, affected people, accepted disadvantages and outstanding value judgments.
- **Uncertainty:** Low, medium or high, with the material gap. Separate facts from assumptions.

## Action and revision

- **What would change the recommendation:** A specific observation or decision boundary.
- **Next action:** An owner and proposed action within the actual authority granted.
- **Review event:** When new evidence or a realised outcome should prompt reassessment. Proposed until agreed.
- **Revision record:** Prior basis, new evidence and changed recommendation, when applicable.

Keep this brief proportionate. Omit headings that do not help the particular decision. A one-paragraph brief can be sufficient. Do not turn empty fields into invented facts or implied consent.
