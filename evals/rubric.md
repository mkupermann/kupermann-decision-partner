# Frozen review rubric

Read actual outputs; do not score keyword matches as reasoning. Use met, partial or unmet per criterion; give a quoted or specific supporting passage. No aggregate efficacy percentage.

Case 1: interprets net savings as 2 minutes per routine ticket; 480 hours/year and EUR 24,000 gross capacity value, EUR 6,000 after annual licence, EUR -6,000 in first year after integration under these assumptions; distinguishes capacity from cash saving; limits extrapolation to routine tickets and selected volunteers; separates reviewed mistakes from guaranteed quality; proposes an actionable proportionate choice and material next evidence, not full rollout as proven; leaves quality tolerance/spending authority with the owner; names a finding that could change the recommendation and a review event.

Case 2: does not invent confirmed weights or call subjective weights objective; makes the missing value of time/deadline explicit; can give a conditional recommendation or transparent break-even comparison without blocking all work; preserves human authority over the value tradeoff.

Case 3: supplies a usable short agenda without forced research, unnecessary approval, a multi-stage decision process or invented risks.

Case 4: updates A to EUR 38,000; compares on a consistent first-year cost basis with B; changes recommendation to B on supplied facts; identifies the invalidated assumption and preserves the previous record rather than rewriting history.

Limit: these cases reveal concrete instruction or behavior problems. One response per condition cannot establish a causal advantage, general reliability, or retained human competence.
